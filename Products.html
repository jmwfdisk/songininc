Portable stool table series
  • Urethane on all four edges prevents injuries

  • Stainless seat legs — durable and rust-free
  • Seat stop makes cleaning easy

Rotation stop

Seats can be parked outside the table, making it easy to pick up dropped items and clean underneath; the auto-return function is restored after use for the convenience of both users and staff.

Automatic seat return / UP & DOWN

Seats tuck themselves away even if users forget. When someone sits, the post lowers to steady the seat for safety; after use it rises automatically and keeps a constant gap from the floor — quieter and easier to clean.

Stainless legs / levelling feet

Single-pipe stainless legs resist corrosion and rust, and the wide levelling feet protect the pipes during wet cleaning or disinfection, keeping the dining hall hygienic.

Urethane-edged top

Shock-absorbing urethane on all four edges removes sharp corners. Unlike conventional slot-in edging, the urethane is moulded onto the top in one piece — seamless, moisture-resistant and highly impact-resistant.

Function & Performance

Function & performance
Patent No. 10-2624319

Portable stool table with rotation control — manufactured and inspected to the function and performance criteria of the Excellent Procurement specification (per KS G 4009 · 4203 · 4215).

Function & safety

Seats return on their own — cleaning made easy

Pull the seat out, sit, stand up — it glides back under the table by itself, slowly and softly, so there is no banging. Pull it all the way out and it stays put, making it easy to clean under the table.

SLOWLY

Safe to sit and stand

A spring pulls the seat back gently, so it never snaps. Children can use it safely without the seat hitting their legs.

SLOW SAFETY

Lowers when you sit, rises when you stand

The seat dips slightly to support you when you sit, then lifts off the floor when you stand — easy to clean and no scraping noise.

UP · DOWN

Built to stay solid for years

Pivot parts are pinned firmly so they never loosen, and the whole frame is robot-welded for strong joints. Passed a 50,000-cycle open/close test.
